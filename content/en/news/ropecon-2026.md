"Beneath the splendor of the industrial revolution and post-war exuberance, metaphysical corruption festers in the shadows as creatures of the Netherworld erode the foundations of a god-forsaken world."

Kustannusosakeyhtiö Gogam is proud to announce that the new urban campaign setting *Welcome to Eevenkoto* will be officially released at Ropecon 2026 on Friday, July 24.

The new publication is a 52-page (including covers) softcover overview of a new roleplaying setting. Eevenkoto offers a gateway to adventures where traditional high fantasy meets industrial upheaval, post-devastating-world-war reconstruction, and the struggle between technology and magic.

## Full 5.5e and L&L Compatibility
The book provides players and game masters with a solid foundation for new adventures, as it is fully compatible with the Legendoja & Lohikäärmeitä system and the latest 5.5e rule updates. Authors **Petri Leinonen** (writing, layout, and illustration) and **Pasi Seppänen** (the adventure *Lost in the Apparatus*) have created a complete package that adapts the rules for a more modern setting and offers new tools for tabletop gaming.

## What's Inside the Book?
The publication serves as a concise introductory book, offering a comprehensive glimpse into the metropolis of Eevenkoto and its rules. The book includes:

- Overview of Eevenkoto: The city's history, social structures, factions, and key districts such as Marble Ridge, Iron Forge, and Market Tunnels.
- Safety and Dialogue Tools: Tools for playing urban and intense drama (including Session 0, X-Card, and managing emotional bleed).
- Character Creation and Rule Variants: Updated rules for handling durabilities, exhaustion, and expertise, among others.
- Cultures and Species: Playable cultures of Eevenkoto include Homebound and Graylander, with species options of Human, Felinoid, and Shapeshifting Troll.
- Backgrounds: New urban backgrounds: Entertainer, Criminal, War Veteran, and Private Investigator.
- Updated Classes: Playable up to 3rd level are revised versions of Daredevil (Barbarian), Scoundrel (Rogue), and Sensation (Bard), including unique cantrips and spells for the Sensation.
- Weapon and Armor Rules: Modernized rules covering firearms, weapon dice chains, and modern protective gear.
- Two Ready-to-Play Adventures: *Kojeeseen kadonnut* (Lost in the Apparatus) by Pasi Seppänen (for 1st-level characters) and *Särkynyt sointu* (Broken Chord) by Petri Leinonen (for 2nd-level characters).

## Availability
*Welcome to Eevenkoto* will be available for purchase starting on release day at Ropecon from the joint Myrrys and Gogam sales booth. Additionally, the book will be available through well-stocked retailers during and after the event.

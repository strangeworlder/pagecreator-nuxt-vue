A new section for downloadable materials has been opened to support the Eevenkoto campaign setting. Two essential, print-ready PDF files are now available for gaming tables: the official Eevenkoto character sheet and the free city map.

## Printable Character Sheet

The Eevenkoto character sheet is an updated version for L&L and 5.5e. The Finnish character sheet can be downloaded and printed in PDF format from the website.

## Eevenkoto City Map

The map familiar from the *Welcome to Eevenkoto* (*Tervetuloa Eevenkotoon*) book is also available as a downloadable PDF that you can print. It is optimized for A4 size, but also works with larger paper sizes.
